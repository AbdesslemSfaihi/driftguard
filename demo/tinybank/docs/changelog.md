# Changelog

Release history. Entries describe what was true when each version shipped.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-01-15

First release.

- Accounts open with a first deposit of 100 EUR or more.
- Withdrawals are limited to 1000 EUR per day.
- Transfers cost 2 EUR and arrive the next business day; instant transfers
  on request.
- Accounts can be closed once their balance is zero.

# Frequently asked questions

Answers for customers, in plain language.

## How much money do I need to open an account?

A first deposit of at least 100 EUR. With less than that, the account is not
opened.

## How much can I take out in one day?

Up to 1000 EUR per day, and never more than you have on the account.

## What does a transfer cost?

2 EUR, paid by the sender on top of the amount sent.

## How long does a transfer take?

A standard transfer arrives the next business day. Ask for an instant
transfer if the money has to arrive right away.

## Can I close my account?

Yes, once the balance is zero. Take out or transfer any remaining money first.
